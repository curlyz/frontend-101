# What LLMs CANNOT Do

- Set realistic expectations about LLM limitations
- Explain why these limitations exist based on how LLMs work
- Help students avoid common pitfalls and misuse

## Content

**Knowledge Limitations**
LLMs have significant knowledge constraints:
- **Knowledge Cutoffs**: They only know information up to their training data cutoff
- **No Real-Time Information**: Cannot access current news, stock prices, or live data
- **No Personal Information**: Cannot remember details about you between conversations
- **Static Knowledge**: Cannot learn or update their knowledge after training

**Mathematical and Logical Reasoning**
LLMs struggle with:
- **Complex Calculations**: May make arithmetic errors or logical mistakes
- **Mathematical Proofs**: Cannot reliably construct valid mathematical arguments
- **Consistent Logic**: May contradict themselves across long conversations
- **Step-by-Step Reasoning**: Can skip steps or make invalid logical jumps

**Real-World Actions**
LLMs cannot:
- **Browse the Internet**: Cannot search for or access live web content
- **Execute Code**: Cannot run programs or access computer systems
- **Control Devices**: Cannot directly interact with hardware or software
- **Make Purchases**: Cannot perform transactions or take real-world actions
- **Send Emails**: Cannot communicate with external systems without integrations

**Memory and Consistency**
LLMs lack:
- **Long-Term Memory**: Cannot remember previous conversations or interactions
- **Persistent Learning**: Cannot improve from individual user interactions
- **Consistent Personality**: May give different responses to the same question
- **Context Across Sessions**: Each conversation starts fresh with no memory

**Understanding vs Pattern Matching**
LLMs don't truly:
- **Understand Meaning**: They recognize patterns but lack genuine comprehension
- **Have Consciousness**: No awareness, emotions, or subjective experiences
- **Learn Like Humans**: Cannot adapt and learn from experience the way humans do
- **Reason About the World**: Cannot form mental models of physical reality

**Reliability Issues**
LLMs may:
- **Hallucinate**: Generate plausible-sounding but completely false information
- **Be Overconfident**: Present uncertain information as if it were fact
- **Exhibit Biases**: Reflect biases present in their training data
- **Produce Inconsistent Results**: Give different answers to the same question

**Professional and Safety Limitations**
LLMs should not be used for:
- **Medical Diagnosis**: Cannot replace professional medical advice
- **Legal Advice**: Cannot provide reliable legal counsel
- **Financial Decisions**: Cannot make investment or financial recommendations
- **Safety-Critical Applications**: Cannot be trusted in life-or-death situations

**Why These Limitations Exist**
These limitations stem from how LLMs work:
- They're trained on static datasets, not live information
- They predict patterns rather than truly reason
- They lack mechanisms for consistent long-term memory
- They cannot verify facts or check their own work

**The Bottom Line**
LLMs are powerful tools with significant limitations. Understanding these limitations is crucial for using them effectively and avoiding potential problems.
